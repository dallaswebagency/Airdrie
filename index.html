How it works

Scrub, rinse, and let it dry clean.

The process is a bit unique but makes total sense when you do the science.

  1. 1

    Scrub with pure water

    We scrub the window trims and glass with a stiff nylon brush, wet with the pure water, to agitate the dirt off the windows through gravity.

  2. 2

    Rinse with only pure water

    Then we thoroughly rinse with only pure water.

  3. 3

    It dries completely clean

    This dries completely clean, because the water is pure.
